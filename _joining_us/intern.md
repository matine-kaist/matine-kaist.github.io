## Position Description

---

As an intern, you will participate in ongoing research projects in the lab. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in research and to contribute to projects in musical acoustics, physics-based sound simulation, and instrument design. The specific research projects and tasks will be determined based on the intern's interests and the lab's needs.

Please review our lab's [research topics](/research/), [demo projects](/demo/), and [publications](/research/publications/) to get a better understanding of our work. We encourage interns to propose their own research ideas and projects, and we will provide guidance and support throughout the internship.


## Current Research Topics

---

We are looking for self-motivated interns who are interested in (but not limited to) the following research topics:

- **Musical Instrument Sound Simulation**
  - <u>Automated Playing Cover Performance</u> Simulator for String Instruments
  - Sound-matched Infill Patterning for [<u>3D-Printed Violins</u>](https://jnwoo.com/projects/2025-violin/)
  - Designing Augmented <u>Tone-modulator</u> (e.g., [FatFinger](https://www.fender.com/products/fatfinger-guitar-sustain-enhancer), [Wolf tone eliminator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_tone))
- **Novel Acoustic Actuator Design**
  - Developing [<u>Dielectric Elastomer</u> for Acoustic Actuation](https://jnwoo.com/projects/2026-slit/)

Any other research topics related to musical acoustics, physics-based sound simulation, and instrument design are also welcome. We encourage interns to propose their own research ideas and projects!


## Privileges as an Intern

---

- Interns will work closely with researchers in the lab, and receive guidance on their research projects.
- Interns will have access to the lab's resources and facilities, and will be encouraged to attend lab meetings and seminars.
- Interns can expect publication and presentation opportunities based on their contributions to the research projects.

## FAQ

---

- Can international students apply for an internship?
    - Yes. KAIST offers [Visiting Student Researcher (VSR) program](https://io.kaist.ac.kr/menu/io.do?mguid=D4CD2D0A-21E5-E511-940C-2C44FD7DF8B9) for international students, through which you can apply for an internship. For international visiting researchers, we recommend planning for a long-term internship (e.g., 6 months) to allow sufficient time for administrative procedures and relocation.
- Can graduate students apply?
    - Yes, graduate students are welcome to apply for an internship. Please note that the primary advisor from your home institution must approve your participation in the internship program.
