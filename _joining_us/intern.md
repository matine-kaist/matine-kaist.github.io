## Position Description

---

As an intern, you will participate in ongoing research projects in the lab. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in research and to contribute to projects in musical acoustics, physics-based sound simulation, and instrument design. The specific research projects and tasks will be determined based on the intern's interests and the lab's needs.


## Expectations

---

- Interns are expected to participate full-time (40 hours per week) during the internship period, unless otherwise arranged.


## Requirements

---

- We occasionally host undergraduate research interns. Positions are competitive and based on research fit.


## Privileges as an Intern

---

- Interns will work closely with researchers in the lab, and receive guidance on their research projects.
- Interns will have access to the lab's resources and facilities, and will be encouraged to attend lab meetings and seminars.
- Interns can expect publication and presentation opportunities based on their contributions to the research projects.


## Application Process

---

- Please send the following to the [PI's email](https://jin-woo-lee.github.io) or to `{{ site.email }}`
  1. A brief description of your research interests and why you want to join the lab.
  2. Your CV / résumé.
  3. Representative publications or a writing sample (if available).
- KAIST students are also encouraged to apply for the [URP Program](https://engineering.kaist.ac.kr/content?menu=61) (Undergraduate Research Participation Program).
- We review applications on a rolling basis and will respond if there is a mutual fit.
