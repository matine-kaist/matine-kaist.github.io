## Position Description

---

As a visiting researcher, you will have the opportunity to collaborate with our lab on ongoing research projects. This position is ideal for individuals who are interested in
participating in our ongoing research projects and/or
contribute to the lab's collaborative research efforts.
You will have the opportunity to work closely with our research team, gain hands-on experience in research, and contribute to projects.
The specific research projects and tasks will be determined based on the visiting researcher's interests and the lab's needs.


## Privileges as a Visiting Researcher

---

- Visiting researchers will work closely with researchers in the lab, and look for mentorship on their research projects from the PI.
- Visiting researchers will have access to the lab's resources and facilities, and will be encouraged to attend lab meetings and seminars.
- Visiting researchers can expect publication and presentation opportunities based on their contributions to the research projects.
