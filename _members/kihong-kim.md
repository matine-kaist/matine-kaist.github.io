Kihong Kim is an M.S. student at the Graduate School of Culture Technology, KAIST, and a member of the Matine Lab. He received his bachelor's degree in Mathematics from Kyungpook National University, with minors in Electronics Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering. His research has focused on identifying and mitigating artifacts in neural audio synthesis, particularly in neural vocoders, and he is currently interested in virtual analog modeling. Outside his current research, he is also curious about new possibilities for creative audio effects.
