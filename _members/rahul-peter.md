Rahul Peter is an MSc student in Acoustics & Audio Technology at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en) and an exchange student at the [School of Computing, KAIST](https://cs.kaist.ac.kr/). His current focus is on modal synthesis, low‑frequency sound field control, and subwoofer performance. Alongside these specific interests, he has worked across a broad range of audio problems and is open to collaborations on anything audio‑related. He got here by starting out in music production as a teenager, taking a detour as a software engineer, and coming back to acoustics.
